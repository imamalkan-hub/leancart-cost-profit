## Long description
LeanCart Cost & Profit solves one daily problem for WooCommerce store owners: "calculating profit margins by hand".
Instead of hundreds of options and bloated code, it is a focused tool that works
right after installation.

### Why buy it?
- **Saves time:** Automates cost and profit calculations without the need for a spreadsheet
- **Minutes to set up:** Works with sensible defaults.
- **Secure:** WordPress CRUD API, nonce and capability checks, proper escaping.
- **Compatible:** WooCommerce 8+, HPOS, PHP 8.0+, WordPress 6.2+.
- **Supported:** 12 months of updates and support, 30-day money-back guarantee.

### Features
* Cost field per product (including variations)
* Profit and percentage column in the product list
* Net profit column per order
* Cost metadata on order line items

### Who is it for?
All WooCommerce stores selling without knowing their profit margins
